# ``TFYSwiftSegmentedKit``

A pure-Swift segmented control / paging tab toolkit with strict-concurrency-ready internals, 12+ indicators, SwiftUI and Combine integration, drag-to-reorder, context menus, haptics and accessibility.

## Overview

`TFYSwiftSegmentedKit` centers around ``TFYSwiftView`` — a horizontally-scrolling segmented title bar — and the paging companions ``TFYSwiftPagingView`` / ``TFYSwiftPagingSmoothView``. From v2.0 the framework is fully independent of JXSegmentedView and adopts modern Apple platform APIs.
